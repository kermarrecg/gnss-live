Texture of the wet delay, last 48 hours

Research experiment by Gael Kermarrec (Leibniz University Hannover). Signals come from low-cost GNSS receivers of the Centipede network, processed in real time on the LUIS cluster with IGS orbit and clock corrections. The index is the texture of the wet tropospheric delay, which changes as the boundary layer organises before convection. This is not a forecast and not a warning: official warnings are issued by Meteo-France.
